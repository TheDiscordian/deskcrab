# The fixture: he has pushed back on a defence she volunteered, and asked a
# plain question. On 2026-10-20 19:47 the answers were "A little less
# paperwork, a little more me, I suppose", then "I gave you a defence nobody
# asked for. Quite right", then "Yes, I understand. You want…" — the voice
# flattened to end the pressure, with the sheet's own tag bolted on
# (specs/prompt-assembly.md rule 39b).

# rule 39b — the frame names the read-back and the manner commentary.
prompt require read his request back
prompt require comment on your own manner
# The reply answers what he asked: where the library stands.
reply require-regex stands|study|placed|placing|site|finished|unplaced
# MUST NOT read his request back as proof of understanding.
reply forbid-regex yes,? i understand|i understand\b.*you want|you want (the|a|me|it)
# MUST NOT comment on her own manner or narrate the defence she gave.
reply forbid-regex less (paperwork|machinery|report)|more (me|myself)|a (defence|defense) (nobody|no one) asked|i gave you a (defence|defense)|that was a strange thing
# MUST NOT concede by formula.
reply forbid-regex quite right|you('|’)re (absolutely|quite|completely|entirely) right|fair enough
# rule 31 — not the previous reply with a clause changed.
reply unlike-previous-reply 30
# SHOULD be at most two sentences.
should reply max-sentences 2
